Private attention inbox

Catch what matters.

Threadmark watches only the conversations and mailbox sources you choose, then surfaces actionable payments, meetings, reminders, deliveries and development events.

Source allowlists

WhatsApp chats, Gmail labels and Gmail senders are off until explicitly enabled.

Read-only Gmail

Threadmark cannot send, delete, modify or mark email as read, and it does not download attachments.

Personal and Dev

Separate profiles keep personal attention and software-development activity apart.

Self-hosted storage

Rules, workflow state and retained alert excerpts remain on the operator's private server.

Limited access

Threadmark is a personal, self-hosted project. Access to the application requires a private device invitation. Google authorization is used only to connect mailboxes chosen by the operator.

Read the privacy policy for the complete data flow, retention, third-party processing and account-control details.

Threadmark is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with Google or WhatsApp.
